HOW IT WORKS

A GUIDE FOR THE OWNERS

The DJ Request Night App turns the brewery's TV into an interactive song queue your patrons control from their phones — they request, they tip to jump the line, and the screen lights up in real time. Built specifically for Inner Groove's vibe and revenue model, it works on any phone with a QR code and runs entirely in a web browser. No app downloads, no logins, no friction.

📺

A live, glowing display of what's coming up next, top tippers, and recently played — designed to look great across the bar.

THE CORE FEATURES

The Tip-to-Bump Economy

The whole thing runs on an honor-system tipping model. Patrons can:

  • ↑
    Bump up — Tip on their own request to jump the line, or tip on someone else's song to push it higher (no more duplicate requests for the same song)
  • ↓
    Bump down — Pay a higher minimum tip to push a song they don't want to hear lower in the queue (creates fun social drama and additional revenue)
  • 💵
    Cash at the bar — Toggle "I'll tip cash" to apply a bump immediately; staff collects when they're at the bar
  • 📲
    Multiple payment methods — Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Zelle, or any custom URL (full charity donation links work too)

Charity Night Mode

💛 BUILT FOR CAUSE-DRIVEN NIGHTS

Flip a single switch in admin to transform the entire app into a fundraiser. The header text changes, a custom message identifies the charity, the TV displays a live $ RAISED counter that grows in real time as patrons tip, and payment methods can be swapped out for the charity's donation links. Reset the counter at the start of each charity event with one button.

The Big Screen Experience

Designed to look great on the brewery TV from across the room:

  • ▶
    Hero "Up Next" card — The next song shown massive, glowing, with the requester's name highlighted
  • 📋
    Coming up queue — The next 6 songs with current tip totals visible to everyone
  • 🏆
    Top tippers leaderboard — Top 3 generous patrons, crowned with a 👑 emoji on the leader (people will compete for this spot)
  • 🎵
    Recently played ticker — Last 5 songs scroll across the bottom so latecomers know what they missed

DJ Console Controls

The PIN-protected admin view gives staff full control:

  • ✓
    One-tap "Played" — Marks a song as played, removes it from the live queue, but keeps it in history for charity totals and the recently-played ticker
  • ⚙️
    Configurable everything — Set minimum bump amounts, swap payment methods, set per-person request limits to prevent the queue from being hogged
  • 🔒
    Public tip totals toggle — Hide tip amounts publicly if the brewery wants to keep things low-key, or show them off to drive the bidding war
  • ↻
    Real-time everywhere — Every device updates simultaneously the moment something changes

WHY THIS IS GOOD FOR THE BREWERY

A NEW REVENUE STREAM

Every tip is incremental income that wouldn't happen otherwise. The bump-down feature in particular adds a fun, voluntary "hate-tax" that drives competitive tipping. Cash tips at the bar also pull patrons up to order another beer.

PATRONS STAY LONGER

Once someone's submitted a request, they're invested in seeing it played. They watch the TV. They order another round while waiting. They pull out their phones again to check the queue.

A CONVERSATION STARTER

The TV becomes a focal point. Patrons compare what they requested. They argue about whether to bump down "Wonderwall." They talk to strangers. The brewery becomes more social.

CHARITY NIGHTS BECOME EFFORTLESS

One toggle turns the entire experience into a fundraiser with live progress on the screen. The visible $ RAISED counter creates social pressure — patrons see the number climbing and want to contribute.

ZERO ONGOING COSTS

The app runs on free-tier hosting (Vercel + Supabase). No monthly subscription, no per-transaction fees, no licensing. The only "cost" is occasionally maintaining or expanding it, which Brian handles.

HOW IT WORKS IN PRACTICE

A typical Friday night:

7:00pm — DJ opens the admin console on a phone or tablet. Confirms the queue is empty from last week. The TV is set to the Display view, glowing in the corner.
7:15pm — First patron scans the QR code at their table. Requests "Don't Stop Believin'." It pops up on the TV instantly as UP NEXT.
8:30pm — Queue has 12 songs. Two patrons each tip $5 to bump their songs higher. A third pays $5 to bump someone else's request down. The TV reflects every change in real time.
9:45pm — Top tippers leaderboard shows three names, with a 👑 next to the leader at $42. They've ordered three more beers tonight than they would have otherwise.
11:00pm — DJ marks the last song as played. Charity night totals (if active) get reset for next time. The system auto-archives everything.

WHAT IT WON'T DO (BY DESIGN)

A few intentional limits worth being honest about:

  • ·
    It doesn't search Spotify or pull album art — Patrons type the song title and artist by hand. This keeps things simple and avoids API costs and failure modes.
  • ·
    It doesn't process payments directly — All tips go through Venmo/Cash App/PayPal on an honor system. The brewery never touches credit card data and there are no transaction fees.
  • ·
    It doesn't enforce who plays what — The DJ always has final say. The queue is a strong suggestion, not a contract.

WHAT'S NEXT IF YOU GREEN-LIGHT IT

The app is live and fully functional today at inner-groove-dj.vercel.app. To go from "tested" to "officially launched," we'd want to:

  1. Lock down the admin PIN (currently a placeholder)
  2. Confirm the real Venmo / Cash App / PayPal handles for the brewery and any recurring charity partners
  3. Print QR codes for each table — small acrylic table-tents work great
  4. Pick a launch night to soft-debut, ideally during a slow shift so the staff can get comfortable with the admin console

From there, additional features can be added based on what works at the bar. The list of possibilities is long and the foundation is solid.
